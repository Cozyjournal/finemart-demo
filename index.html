How to Order

  1. Browse the categories above, or search for what you need.
  2. Tap “Add to Cart” on every item — change quantities in the cart.
  3. Press “Order on WhatsApp” — your full order is typed for you. Add your name and address, and press send.
  4. Pay cash when your order arrives at your door.

About Fine Mart

Fine Mart is your neighbourhood online grocery store: daily bread and bakery, baby food and formula, pasta, rusk and everyday essentials — all in one place, with home delivery. Our aim is simple: the items your home needs every day, at fair prices, without you having to visit the market.

Common Questions

How do I place an order?

Add your items to the cart and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. Your order list, quantities and total are prepared automatically — just add your name and address and send.

Do you deliver at home?

Yes — home delivery is available. Place your order here or on WhatsApp and it will be brought to your door.

How do I pay?

You can pay cash when your order is delivered to you.
